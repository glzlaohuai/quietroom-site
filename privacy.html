Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Summary

QuietRoom is published by hiimobapps. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics, and there is no QuietRoom server. The app does not collect personal data.

Data on your device

Sounds are bundled with the app. Your rooms, settings and the latest valid backup are stored only in the app’s local container on your iPhone. There is no cloud sync. Device backups you make through Apple may include this app data according to your own settings.

Deleting the app removes its rooms from your device.

Head tracking and motion

When you turn on head tracking with compatible headphones, orientation is processed locally by Apple’s audio system. QuietRoom does not record, store or upload head-pose or motion history. Declining Motion access does not prevent ordinary playback.

Purchases

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple through StoreKit under Apple’s terms. QuietRoom receives only the verified status of your purchase and never sees payment-card details. Restoring on a new installation needs an internet connection.

Permissions

QuietRoom does not request microphone, location, contacts, photos, health or advertising-tracking access.

Children

QuietRoom does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
