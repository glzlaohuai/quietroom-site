A little quiet, right here.

QuietRoom places nature sounds around you: rain on the window to your left, a stream ahead, a fire just behind. Put on headphones, start listening, and shape the room until it feels right.

Everything a quiet room needs

Sounds in real space

Drag each sound around a 3D listener: left or right, in front or behind, above or below. Or set direction, elevation and distance precisely. Wide backgrounds stay steady around you.

36 natural sounds

Rain, thunder, birds, streams, waves, wind, insects, footsteps and fire. Recordings vary naturally, so the room never feels like a short loop. Preview any sound, Pro included, before you add it.

Occasional sounds, your pace

Thunder, birdsong, drips and footsteps arrive now and then. Choose Rare, Normal or Frequent for each one. Footsteps can also walk continuously.

Rooms of your own

Start from Rainy study, Forest camp or Seaside cabin, or begin with a blank room. Changes save automatically; rename, duplicate or replace saved rooms anytime.

Head tracking

With compatible headphones, spatial sounds stay put as you turn your head. Regular headphones still give fixed spatial sound.

Offline, private, calm

No account, no ads, no analytics. Sounds are bundled, so listening, editing and saving work without a connection. A sleep timer fades out gently.
