Everything a quiet room needs

Sounds in real space

Drag each spatial source around a 3D listener, or set direction, height and distance precisely. Wide ambience stays steady around you.

36 natural sounds

Rain, thunder, birds, streams, waves, wind, insects, footsteps and fire, with recordings that vary naturally so the room never feels like a short loop.

Occasional sounds, your pace

Thunder, birdsong, drips and footsteps arrive now and then. Choose rare, normal or frequent for each one.

Listen before you add

Preview any sound in the library, including Pro sounds, for as long as you like before adding it to your room.

Head tracking

With compatible headphones, spatial sources stay put as you turn your head. Regular headphones still give fixed spatial sound.

Offline, private, calm

No account, no ads, no analytics. Sounds are bundled, so listening, editing and saving work without a connection. A sleep timer fades out gently.
