Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings (the ··· button) or the Pro page and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. A new installation needs an internet connection the first time.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. QuietRoom Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Refunds follow Apple’s policies.

How do I make my own room?

Tap the room title to open Rooms. Choose Add new room to start blank, or Save current room to keep your current mix. Edits save automatically. Free includes one room of your own; Pro allows twenty.

How do I move a sound up, down or behind me?

Drag its icon around the head; you can move it above, below or behind you. For exact values, tap the sound and set Direction, Elevation and Distance. Drag the head itself to look at the room from another angle; that does not change what you hear.

Head tracking says it is unavailable.

Head tracking needs headphones that support it, spatial audio enabled for the current output, and Motion access for QuietRoom. It starts while a room is playing. Regular headphones still give fixed spatial sound, and speakers give basic playback.

How long does a sound preview last?

As long as you like. A preview plays until you tap Stop, close the sound library or leave the app. Any sound can be previewed, including Pro sounds. A playing room lowers its volume during the preview.

Thunder or birds come too rarely, or too often.

Tap the sound in your room and choose Rare, Normal or Frequent under How often. Footsteps also offer occasional steps or a continuous walk.

Does QuietRoom play in the background?

Yes. Playback continues on the lock screen and in the background, with lock-screen controls. Turn on Mix with other audio in Settings to play alongside other apps.

How does the sleep timer work?

Choose 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes. The room fades out over the last ten seconds and stops. Pausing manually turns the timer off.

Where are my rooms stored? How do I delete them?

Only on your iPhone. Delete a room from Rooms → ··· → Delete, or delete the app to remove everything.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Head tracking requires compatible headphones and Motion access.

Digital volume is not a measure of headphone sound pressure. Please listen at a comfortable level.
